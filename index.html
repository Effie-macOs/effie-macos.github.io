Effie for Mac

Effie is a light, clean, yet powerful markup-based writing and mind-mapping app. Designed for note-taking, brainstorming, list-making, and committed long-form writing. It bridges the gap between ideas and execution with minimal distraction and seamless tools.

Minimal Interface

Focus is everything. Effie strips away distractions, leaving just you, your thoughts, and the page. No unnecessary features. Pure writing flow.

Mind Maps Meet Markup

Visualize ideas as mind maps, then switch to editor with one click. Changes sync instantly. Turn unordered lists into structured maps—or vice versa—without leaving your workflow.

Seamless Sync Across Devices

Works on Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Windows. Real-time cloud sync keeps everything accessible, wherever inspiration strikes. Autosave ensures nothing is lost.

Privacy First

Your data is encrypted with a unique key. No ads, no tracking, no access by anyone else. Write freely, securely.

Effortless Features

Simplified Markdown with / insight panel. Export to PDF, DOCX, Markdown, EffieSheet, or JPEG. Themes for light or dark. Mind map styles: Fishbones, Logic Chart, Matrix, Timeline, Org Chart. Native code for low resource use.

How It Works

1. Capture Ideas

Start with notes, lists, or brainstorming in the clean editor. Use Markdown for quick formatting—no mouse needed.

2. Visualize and Organize

One tap converts lists to mind maps. Explore connections with styles like Timeline or Org Chart. Edits sync both ways.

3. Write and Refine

Switch to full editor. Autosave and real-time sync across devices. Stay immersive, distraction-free.

4. Export and Share

Export polished work to PDF, DOCX, or more. Access from anywhere, on any device.
